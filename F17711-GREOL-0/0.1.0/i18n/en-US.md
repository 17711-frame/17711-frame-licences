# F17711-GREOL — English summary (0.1.0)

Core mandates include attribution, share-alike, and adherence to ethical values such as human rights, equity, collective and shared progress, non-exclusion, future generations and transmission, a safe planet, and privacy. The license also emphasizes minimizing energy consumption. It explicitly forbids enclosing the commons, violating privacy, using the work for extractive, deceptive, or harmful control systems, supporting any form of slavery, or harming the planet and future generations.

When distributing the work or its derivatives, an AI Manifest must be included, detailing the date of AI interaction (ISO 8601), the use of generative AI, LLM model (optional), and whether it was human-reviewed, tested, or corrected. It is recommended to use a separate file named `AI_MANIFEST.md` for this purpose.
